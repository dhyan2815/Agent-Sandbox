# Settlements

A settlement is the transfer of captured payments from Lumen Pay to your bank account.

## Settlement schedule

Captured payments are settled to your bank account on a T+2 schedule, which means two business days after the capture date. Settlements are not processed on bank holidays.

## Fees

Lumen Pay charges a platform fee of 2 percent on every captured payment. The fee is deducted before settlement, so the settled amount is the captured amount minus the fee and any refunds.

## Settlement reports

Download a settlement report from the dashboard or fetch it from the /settlements endpoint. Each report lists the payments, refunds and fees included in the settlement.

## Instant settlements

Instant settlements transfer money within 15 minutes of capture and are available to accounts older than 90 days.
