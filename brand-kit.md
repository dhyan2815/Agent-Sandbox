## 01 · BRAND STRATEGY

### Who You Are
You are a LinkedIn content creator who makes AI and automation accessible — for beginners who feel overwhelmed and intermediates who want to go deeper. You don't teach theory. You show systems that work. Your content moves people from confusion to clarity, from tools to real workflows.

### Core Metaphor
**Signal → System**

Your brand is about turning noise into signal, and signal into working systems. AI is chaotic and fast-moving. You are the calm translator. Every post you write is a signal — clear, purposeful, actionable.

This metaphor drives everything: your logo, your slide layouts, your tone, your color choices.

### Brand Personality
- **Calm, not cold.** You are approachable, not corporate.
- **Precise, not dense.** Short sentences. Clear ideas. No filler.
- **Educational, not performative.** You teach, you don't flex.
- **Modern, not trendy.** You use current tools but your brand doesn't chase aesthetics — it holds steady.

### Taglines (use interchangeably)
- **Primary:** `From tools to systems — AI that works.`
- **Secondary:** `AI & Automation. Simplified.`
- **Micro (for footers/bylines):** `Tools → Systems.`

### Audience
- **Beginners** in AI/automation — curious, intimidated, early in their journey.
- **Intermediates** — building things, want structure, want to go from scattered to systematic.
- **Gujarat-based tech community + Indian AI/ML students** who want practical, real-world education without jargon overload.

### What Your Brand Must Never Feel Like
- Overhyped ("AI will change everything!!! 🚀🚀🚀")
- Corporate or cold
- Dense or academic
- Like every other AI influencer page

## 02 · COLOR SYSTEM

### Palette Name: **Clean Signal**

This palette is built on the "clean + minimal + editorial" vibe with Electric Blue/Cyan as the anchor accent. It feels trustworthy, modern, and tech-forward without screaming startup.

```
BASE COLORS
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Background (Primary)    #F5F6F8    Soft Off-White / Paper
Background (Dark Alt)   #0D0F12    Near-Black (for dark slides)
Surface / Cards         #FFFFFF    Pure White
Text Primary            #0D0F12    Near-Black
Text Secondary          #6B7280    Cool Gray

ACCENT COLORS
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Accent Blue (Primary)   #2563EB    Electric Blue — CTA, highlights, active elements
Accent Cyan (Secondary) #06B6D4    Cyan — tags, chips, hover states, icon fills
Accent Muted            #DBEAFE    Pale Blue — slide background tints, code blocks

NEUTRAL SYSTEM
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Border / Divider        #E2E8F0    Light Gray
Muted Surface           #F1F5F9    Subtle card background
Code Block BG           #1E293B    Slate Dark (for code snippets)
```

### Color Rules
1. **One accent per slide.** Use either `#2563EB` OR `#06B6D4` — not both together unless one is a tint.
2. **Backgrounds stay neutral.** Off-white `#F5F6F8` for most slides. Near-black `#0D0F12` only for cover slides or section breaks.
3. **Accent must appear at least once per slide.** In a border, a chip, a highlighted word, or an icon.
4. **Never use:** random gradients, neon glow, rainbow palettes, or purple-blue AI clichés.
5. **Code/terminal blocks** always use `#1E293B` with `#06B6D4` for syntax highlights.

### Palette in Practice
```
Cover slide         → Dark BG (#0D0F12) + White text + Blue accent chip
Content slides      → White/Off-white BG + Dark text + Cyan accent details
Highlight slides    → Pale Blue (#DBEAFE) BG + Blue text + no accent needed
Code slides         → Slate dark (#1E293B) BG + Cyan highlights
Last slide / CTA    → Dark BG (#0D0F12) + White + Blue CTA button
```

## 03 · TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

### Font Stack

```
PRIMARY TYPEFACE (Headlines)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Font:       Inter
Weight:     700 (Bold) for h1, 600 (SemiBold) for h2
Style:      No italics on headlines
Use for:    Slide titles, section headers, big callout text
Fallback:   system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif

SECONDARY TYPEFACE (Body / Explanation)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Font:       Inter
Weight:     400 (Regular), 500 (Medium) for emphasis
Use for:    Body text, bullet points, descriptions
Line height: 1.6× for readability

MONOSPACE (Code / Technical)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Font:       JetBrains Mono (preferred) / Fira Code / Courier New
Weight:     400 Regular
Use for:    Code blocks, tool names, commands, technical labels
Accent:     #06B6D4 (cyan) for keywords

MICRO LABELS (Footer, Tags, Handles)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Font:       Inter
Weight:     400 Regular
Size:       10–12px
Color:      #6B7280 (Cool Gray)
Case:       Uppercase, letter-spacing: 0.08em
Use for:    Slide numbers, @dhyan2815, section labels
```

### Type Scale for Carousel Slides

```
Slide Title (h1)      → 28–36px · Bold · #0D0F12 (or White on dark)
Subheading (h2)       → 20–24px · SemiBold · #0D0F12
Body Text             → 15–16px · Regular · #0D0F12
Caption / Label       → 11–13px · Regular Uppercase · #6B7280
Code                  → 14px · JetBrains Mono · #06B6D4 on #1E293B
Accent Callout        → 18–22px · SemiBold · #2563EB
```

### Typography Rules
1. **Maximum 2 font weights per slide.** Don't mix Bold + Medium + Light all at once.
2. **Headlines lead. Body follows.** The hierarchy must be obvious at a glance — even at mobile thumbnail size.
3. **No all-caps headlines.** Only micro-labels (footer, tags) use uppercase.
4. **Never justify text.** Left-align everything. Ragged right edge is cleaner.
5. **Line length max = ~60 characters.** Beyond that, split into two lines.

## 04 · LOGO / AVATAR MARK

### Logo Concept: **DP Signal Node**

Your personal mark uses two ideas fused:

- **DP Monogram** — your initials, reduced to a clean geometric form
- **Signal Node** — a small dot or orbital circle that implies connection, path, and clarity

**The mark reads:** "This is a human sending clear signal."

### Logo Construction Logic
```
Shape:    Square or circle container (rounded corners, radius = 20% of size)
BG:       #0D0F12 (near-black)
Letter:   D + P, interlocked or stacked — clean, geometric, no serifs
Accent:   A single cyan dot (#06B6D4) placed at the terminal point of the P
           — this dot = the signal. It's the one detail that makes it ownable.
Clearspace: Minimum 16px on all sides when placed on a slide
```

### Logo Usage
```
Profile picture    → Square mark, dark BG, cyan dot accent
Slide footer       → Small wordmark: "Dhyan Patel" in Inter 500 + @dhyan2815 in gray
Cover slide        → Large mark, centered, dark background slide
Watermark (light)  → 15% opacity mark placed bottom-right on content slides
```

### What to Actually Use (Practical)
Since this is a text-based brand kit:

1. **For Canva/Figma:** Use a dark square container, type "DP" in Inter Bold, add a small filled circle in `#06B6D4` at the bottom-right of the "P" stroke.
2. **For quick use:** The wordmark **"Dhyan Patel"** in Inter 700, `#0D0F12`, with `· @dhyan2815` appended in `#6B7280` works as a functional brand signature.
3. **Avoid:** Clip-art logos, AI-generated sparkle marks, gradient badge generators.

## 05 · CAROUSEL SLIDE SYSTEM

This is the core of your brand kit — how every carousel you create should be structured.

### Slide Dimensions
```
Format:       1080 × 1080px (Square — standard LinkedIn carousel)
Margin:       56px on all sides (safe zone)
Gutter:       24px between content columns if using split layout
Max columns:  2 (rarely), default is full-width single column
```

### Slide Templates

---

#### SLIDE TYPE 1 — Cover Slide
**Purpose:** Stop the scroll. State the topic. Signal the value.

```
Background:     #0D0F12 (Dark)
Accent chip:    Small pill/tag in #2563EB — e.g. "AI Basics" or "n8n" or "RAG"
Headline:       28–36px · Inter Bold · White · Max 8 words
Subline:        Optional. 15px · Inter Regular · #6B7280 · One line only
Footer:         Bottom-left: "Dhyan Patel · @dhyan2815" in 11px gray
                Bottom-right: Slide count "01 / 08" in 11px gray
Accent detail:  Single thin horizontal rule (1px, #2563EB, 40px wide) above the headline
```

**Example:**
```
[Tag chip: LLM Basics]

How RAG Actually Works
(and why most tutorials get it wrong)

────────────────────────────────
Dhyan Patel · @dhyan2815          01 / 08
```

---

#### SLIDE TYPE 2 — Concept / Definition Slide
**Purpose:** Define one term or idea. Clarity first.

```
Background:     #FFFFFF (White)
Top label:      Small uppercase gray text — "CONCEPT" or "WHAT IS THIS"
Headline:       The term being defined · 28px Bold
Divider:        1px line in #E2E8F0
Body:           2–4 lines max · 15px Regular · #0D0F12
Accent word:    1–2 words bolded in #2563EB within body text
Footer:         Same format — name + handle + slide number
```

---

#### SLIDE TYPE 3 — Step / How-To Slide
**Purpose:** Teach one step of a process.

```
Background:     #F5F6F8 (Off-White)
Step indicator: Large number — "03" in 64px · Inter Bold · #DBEAFE (pale, background)
                Overlaid with step label in #2563EB · 13px Uppercase
Headline:       Step title · 24px SemiBold · #0D0F12
Body:           2–3 bullet points max · 14px Regular
                Each bullet: "→" prefix in Cyan (#06B6D4)
Code (if any):  Inline code in #1E293B box · Cyan text · JetBrains Mono 13px
Footer:         Same
```

---

#### SLIDE TYPE 4 — Code / Technical Slide
**Purpose:** Show a snippet, command, or tool config.

```
Background:     #0D0F12 (Dark) or #1E293B (Slate)
Label:          Small pill: "CODE" or tool name (n8n, Python, LangChain)
                In #06B6D4 · 11px mono uppercase
Code block:     Centered or full-width
                Font: JetBrains Mono 13–14px
                Keywords: #06B6D4 (Cyan)
                Strings: #86EFAC (Soft green)
                Comments: #6B7280 (Gray)
                Background: slightly lighter than slide BG
Annotation:     Optional 1–2 line explanation below in 13px gray
Footer:         White text on dark · Same format
```

---

#### SLIDE TYPE 5 — Comparison / Before-After Slide
**Purpose:** Show contrast. Old way vs new way. Wrong vs right.

```
Background:     #FFFFFF
Layout:         Two columns, separated by 1px divider in #E2E8F0
Left col:       Label "BEFORE" in gray uppercase · content in regular weight
Right col:      Label "AFTER" in #2563EB uppercase · content in SemiBold
Accent:         Right column has a thin left border in #06B6D4 (2px)
Footer:         Standard
```

---

#### SLIDE TYPE 6 — Highlight / Callout Slide
**Purpose:** A big idea. One sentence. Max impact.

```
Background:     #DBEAFE (Pale Blue) OR #0D0F12 (Dark)
Content:        Single statement · 24–32px · Inter Bold · centered
                Max 15 words
Accent line:    Short 40px rule in #2563EB above the text
Attribution:    "— Dhyan Patel" in 13px gray below
Footer:         Standard (smaller, low contrast on pale blue BG)
```

---

#### SLIDE TYPE 7 — Tool Spotlight Slide
**Purpose:** Introduce or explain a specific tool (n8n, LangChain, Claude API, etc.)

```
Background:     #F5F6F8
Top section:    Tool name in 28px Bold + short 1-line description
                Tool "category" chip in #06B6D4 (e.g. "Automation" "LLM" "RAG")
Middle:         3 bullet points — what it does, who it's for, one real use case
Bottom strip:   Thin bar in #2563EB at bottom, with tool URL or "learn more" label
Footer:         Standard
```

---

#### SLIDE TYPE 8 — Last Slide / CTA
**Purpose:** Tell them what to do next. One clear action.

```
Background:     #0D0F12 (Dark)
Headline:       "Found this useful?" or the post topic recap · 22px · White
CTA:            One action in 18px SemiBold · #06B6D4
                e.g. "Follow @dhyan2815 for more AI breakdowns."
                     "Save this for your next project."
                     "Comment your question below."
Logo mark:      DP mark or wordmark — centered, larger than footer usage
Footer:         "Dhyan Patel · @dhyan2815 · AI & Automation Educator"
                in 11px gray · bottom center
```

## 06 · SLIDE RHYTHM RULES

Every carousel must follow a rhythm. Not every slide should be equally loud.

```
Slide 01   → Cover           (LOUD — hook)
Slide 02   → Context/Why     (quiet — setup)
Slide 03   → Step or Concept (functional)
Slide 04   → Step or Code    (technical)
Slide 05   → Step or Compare (functional)
Slide 06   → Key Insight     (LOUD — callout)
Slide 07   → Tool/Resource   (functional)
Slide 08   → Last/CTA        (LOUD — close)
```

Rules:
- Never put two dark slides back-to-back (except cover → context is okay).
- The callout slide (Type 6) should always appear in the second half.
- The last slide must always be dark with a CTA.
- Recommended carousel length: **6–10 slides**. Never more than 12.

## 07 · VOICE & WRITING TONE FOR SLIDES

Your brand voice on slides must match your content voice.

```
✅ DO                              ❌ DON'T
─────────────────────────────     ──────────────────────────────────
Short sentences                   Long dense paragraphs on a slide
Plain English                     Technical jargon without explanation
Active voice                      Passive constructions
Real examples                     Hypothetical "imagine if..." setups
"Here's what I found."            "In today's rapidly evolving landscape..."
Numbers when possible             Vague claims
One idea per slide                Two competing ideas on one slide
"→" as a bullet prefix            Generic dash bullets
```

### Sentence Templates for Slide Copy
```
Concept slides:
  "[Tool/Term] = [Plain English explanation]."
  "Most people think [X]. It's actually [Y]."

Step slides:
  "Step [N]: [Action verb] + [what]."
  "Before you [do X], you need [Y]."

Callout slides:
  "The [tool/concept] isn't the hard part. [The real challenge] is."
  "If you only remember one thing from this: [statement]."

CTA slides:
  "Follow @dhyan2815 if you want more [topic] breakdowns."
  "Save this. You'll need it when you start building [X]."
```

## 09 · ANTI-PATTERNS — WHAT NEVER TO DO

These kill brand consistency and credibility. Avoid always.

```
VISUAL ANTI-PATTERNS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
❌ Random emoji sprinkled into slides
❌ Multiple fonts (more than Inter + JetBrains Mono)
❌ Gradient backgrounds (use flat colors only)
❌ Drop shadows on text
❌ Clipart or stock icons
❌ More than 2 accent colors on one slide
❌ Centered body text (center only callout slides)
❌ Text smaller than 13px (unreadable on mobile)
❌ Slide backgrounds in random colors (stick to the 5-color system)

COPY ANTI-PATTERNS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
❌ Buzzword openers ("In today's AI-driven world...")
❌ Hype without substance ("This will change EVERYTHING")
❌ Walls of text on one slide
❌ More than 4 bullet points per slide
❌ Vague CTAs ("Like and share!")
```
